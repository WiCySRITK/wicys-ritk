About WiCyS

What is WiCyS?

Women in CyberSecurity (WiCyS) is a nonprofit organization dedicated to the recruitment, retention, and advancement of women in cybersecurity.

Organization

The Global WiCyS Organization

WiCyS was founded in 2012 with the goal of bringing together women in cybersecurity from academia, research, and industry. Today, it has grown into a global force for diversity and inclusion in the cybersecurity field.

"To bring together women in cybersecurity for recruitment, retention, and advancement in the field."

WiCyS serves as a catalyst for change, creating pathways for women to enter, thrive, and lead in cybersecurity careers through conferences, scholarships, training, and a vibrant global community.

🎯 Mission

To recruit, retain, and advance women in cybersecurity — creating a diverse, inclusive, and highly skilled cybersecurity workforce for the future.

👁️ Vision

A world where gender is no barrier to excellence and leadership in cybersecurity — where every woman has the opportunity and support to reach her full potential.

Our Story

Our Founding Story

2026 — Founded
The Chapter is Born

A small group of passionate students at RIT Kosovo recognized the need for a dedicated community supporting women in cybersecurity on campus. With the backing of faculty and the national WiCyS organization, our chapter was officially founded.

2023 — Growth
First Major Events & Partnerships

Membership doubled in our second year. We hosted our first guest speaker series and formed partnerships with local tech companies and cybersecurity firms, creating real career pathways for our members.

2024 — Expansion
National Recognition & Competitions

Our team represented RIT Kosovo at the WiCyS National Conference. Members began competing in national CTF competitions, and our chapter received recognition for community engagement from the university.

2025–2026 — Today
A Thriving Community

With over 50 active members, a robust events calendar, and industry connections across the region, our chapter continues to grow and make a lasting impact at RIT Kosovo and in the wider Balkan tech ecosystem.
